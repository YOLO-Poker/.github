### What We Do

YoLo Poker Club sits at the intersection of two worlds:

> **AI-Driven Poker Technology** — Research, develop, and refine intelligent poker agents powered by machine learning, Monte-Carlo tree search, and real-time table analysis.

> **Free-to-Play Poker Communities** — Operate active, zero-stakes clubs across popular poker platforms so players of every level can practice, experiment, and compete without risking real money.

We believe the best poker minds are forged through repeated play against real opponents — and accelerated by AI that never sleeps, never tilts, and never folds.

### 🤖 AI Poker Engine

Our research and engineering pipeline focuses on building autonomous poker agents capable of high-level decision-making across multiple variants.

| Capability | Description |
|---|---|
| **Real-Time Table OCR** | Computer-vision pipelines that read live tables, detect hole cards, community cards, bet sizes, and player actions in milliseconds. |
| **Game-Solving Foundations** | Strategies grounded in counterfactual regret minimisation (CFR) and Nash equilibrium approximations, adapted to incomplete-information settings. |
| **Opponent Modelling** | Adaptive player profiles that track VPIP, PFR, 3-bet %, bluff frequency, and position-based tendencies across thousands of hands. |
| **Multi-Strategy Blending** | A hybrid decision layer that routes each street through pre-flop GTO, post-flop heuristic, and Monte-Carlo roll-out modules before selecting an action. |
| **Hand History Pipeline** | Ingestion, parsing, and structured storage of hand logs for retrospective analysis, model fine-tuning, and report generation. |

### 🃏 Free-to-Play Poker Clubs

We run active, play-money clubs on leading poker platforms. No deposits. No cash-outs. Just real opponents and real poker.

| Platform | Club | Description |
|---|---|---|
| [PPPoker](https://yolopokerclub.com/pppoker) | **Yolo Poker Club** | Multiplayer tables that run around the clock. Play against real humans, discuss hands, and learn together. |
| [ClubGG](https://yolopokerclub.com/clubgg) | **Yolo Poker** | A mobile-first, subscription-style private club with free Gold Coins and a low-pressure environment for strategy experimentation. |

**Why play-money clubs?**

- **Risk-free strategy lab** — Test new opening ranges, bluffing lines, and post-flop plays without financial consequences.
- **Real opponents, real decisions** — No AI filler. Every seat is filled by an actual player making live choices.
- **Same table, same people** — Regulars build familiarity. You develop reads that transfer to real-money play.
- **24/7 activity** — Especially on PPPoker, tables stay active day and night across time zones.

### How to Join

1. **PPPoker** — Install the app, join club ID **`[JOIN_ID]`**, or search "YoLo Poker".
2. **ClubGG** — Search for "YoLo Poker" in the club directory, or follow the [ClubGG page](https://yolopokerclub.com/clubgg) for the latest invite link.

> Chips have **no real-world value**. Our clubs exist purely for practice, community, and skill development.
